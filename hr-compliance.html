What surveyors check

Every staff file. Every requirement.

Accreditation and licensing reviews look closely at personnel files. One missing document across 40 staff turns into a finding. We check each file for items like:

  • Current professional licenses & certifications
  • Required trainings completed & documented
  • Background checks on file
  • Health screenings such as TB tests, where required
  • CPR / first aid certifications
  • Signed job descriptions
  • Orientation & onboarding records
  • Annual evaluations & competency checks
  • Renewal dates tracked before they lapse
  • Offboarding & system access removal

Requirements vary by state, accreditor and level of care. We work from your applicable standards, and you confirm the final requirements.

Questions

Good to know

Do you replace our compliance consultant or HR attorney?

No. We handle the hands-on HR and staff compliance work: files, tracking, follow-up and hiring logistics. Regulatory interpretation and legal questions stay with your compliance lead or attorney, and we're glad to work alongside them.

Who decides what's required?

We work from the standards that apply to you (state licensing, your accreditor and your own policies). Your leadership confirms the final requirements and approves every change.

Why start with an audit?

Every center is different. The audit shows exactly what needs fixing, so the cleanup quote is based on real work rather than a guess. The audit fee is credited toward the cleanup.

Will you access patient information?

Staff file work rarely involves patient records. If any task does involve PHI, we sign a BAA first and use minimum-necessary access.

Who makes hiring decisions?

You do. We run the process (posting, screening, scheduling and paperwork) so you only spend time on the best candidates.

Our survey is soon. Can you help?

Often, yes. Rush timelines are available. Book a call and tell us your date.
