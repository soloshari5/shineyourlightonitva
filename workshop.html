The 45 minutes

What we'll cover

  1. 01

    The 5 back-office leaks

    Unanswered patient messages, no-shows, lapsed staff credentials, stale policies and slow admissions: how to spot each one, and what it's quietly costing you.

  2. 02

    What to delegate first

    A simple way to decide which tasks only you can do and which ones a trained team should own, starting this month.

  3. 03

    Delegating without HIPAA risk

    BAAs, minimum-necessary access, offshoring red flags and the questions to ask any VA or agency before they touch PHI.

  4. 04

    A 30-day handoff plan

    The exact sequence for moving work off your plate without disrupting patients or staff.

  5. 05

    Live Q&A

    Bring your messiest operational problem and we'll work through it together.

This is for you if
  • You own or run a behavioral health program, dental practice or physician practice
  • You're still handling patient calls, scheduling or compliance tracking yourself
  • You've thought about a VA but worry about HIPAA, quality or offshoring
  • You want your evenings back
This isn't for you if
  • You already have a fully staffed, well-run operations team
  • You're looking for the cheapest hourly help available
  • You're not open to changing how things are done
