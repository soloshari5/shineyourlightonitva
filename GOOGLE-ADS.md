# Google Ads Campaign — Hire a VA

Everything here is ready to paste into Google Ads. Replace `YOURDOMAIN.com` with your real web address (for example `shineyourlightonitva.vercel.app` until you have your own domain).

## Campaign settings

| Setting | Choose |
|---|---|
| Goal | **Leads** (or "Create a campaign without a goal's guidance") |
| Campaign type | **Search** |
| Networks | **Uncheck** Search Partners and Display Network |
| Locations | United States — choose **"Presence: people in or regularly in your locations"** |
| Language | English |
| Budget | **$25/day** to start (about $750/month) |
| Bidding | Start with **Maximize clicks** with a max CPC limit of **$12**. Switch to **Maximize conversions** after you have 15+ tracked bookings |
| Ad schedule | Monday–Friday, 7am–7pm (your time zone) |
| Auto-applied recommendations | **Turn off** (Tools → Recommendations → Auto-apply) |

**How the $25/day splits:** Google sets the budget per campaign, not per ad group. To control the split, create **two campaigns**:

| Campaign | Ad groups | Daily budget |
|---|---|---|
| **Healthcare VA** | Group 1 | $15 |
| **Hire a VA** | Group 2 + Group 3 | $10 |

## Tracking bookings (set this up before spending)

Without this, Google can't tell which keywords lead to calls.

1. **Google Ads → Goals → Conversions → New conversion action → Website.**
2. Name it **"Booked call"**, category **Book appointment**.
3. Google gives you a **tag ID** (looks like `AW-123456789`) and a **conversion label**. Send both to Claude, and the code will be added to your pages.
4. If your Calendly plan includes the **Google Analytics / Google Ads integration**, connect it too. It counts actual bookings, not just button clicks.

## What to check, and when

| When | Look at | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Day 3 | Are ads showing? Any clicks? | If no impressions, raise max CPC to $15 |
| Week 1 | **Search terms** report | Add irrelevant searches as negative keywords |
| Week 2 | Clicks and cost per click by group | Pause keywords with lots of clicks and no calls |
| Week 4 | **Cost per booked call** | Raise the budget 20–30% on whatever books calls. Cut Group 3 if it brings nothing |

**Rough first-month expectation:** about 60–150 clicks and a few booked calls. One signed client at $3,500/month covers years of this budget.

---

## Policy reminders

- Say **"HIPAA-aware"** or **"BAA signed."** Avoid "HIPAA certified" — no such certification exists, and Google may flag unverifiable claims.
- No exclamation marks in headlines, no ALL CAPS words, and no phone numbers in the ad text (use a call asset instead).
