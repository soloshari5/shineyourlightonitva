Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Short version: we collect what you give us when you reach out, we use it to talk to you, and we don't sell it. The details are below.

Please don't put patient information in our forms. This website is not set up to receive protected health information (PHI). Don't include patient names, diagnoses, or any other patient details in the Personality Match, a contact form, or an email until a Business Associate Agreement is signed. See HIPAA & BAA Info.

Who we are

Shine Your Light On It ("we," "us") is a US-based virtual assistant and operations support business, run as a sole proprietorship. This policy covers shineyourlightonit.com and our related landing pages (the "Site"). Questions about it go to shineyourlightonit@gmail.com.

What we collect

What you give us

  • Personality Match and contact forms: your name, email address, phone number, business or practice name, website, the type of practice you run, your answers to the fit questions, and whether you checked the BAA acknowledgment.
  • Booking a call: if you book through Calendly, Calendly collects your name, email, and whatever else you enter there. That data is handled under Calendly's own privacy policy.
  • Email: anything you send us directly.

What the Site collects automatically

  • Technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device, the pages you visit, how long you stay, and the page that sent you to us.
  • Cookies and similar technologies that help us understand traffic and measure our advertising (see "Cookies, analytics, and ads" below).

How we use it

  • To reply to you, review your fit answers, and set up a call.
  • To decide whether we're the right partner for your business, and to prepare a proposal if we are.
  • To run, secure, and improve the Site.
  • To measure which ads and pages bring in the right people, and to show our ads to people who might want them.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't use what you submit on the Site to make automated decisions that have legal effects on you. The Personality Match is a fit check that a real person reads.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information for money. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed:

  • EmailJS delivers form submissions to our inbox and sends you a confirmation email.
  • Calendly handles call scheduling.
  • Google provides fonts and icons for the Site, and may provide analytics and advertising services (Google Analytics, Google Ads).
  • LinkedIn may provide ad measurement through the LinkedIn Insight Tag.
  • jsDelivr, a content delivery network, serves a script the Site needs to run. It sees your IP address when it does.
  • Our email and business tools (for example, our email provider and document storage) where your messages and files end up.
  • Authorities and advisors if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, and our attorneys and accountants as needed.
  • A buyer or successor if the business is ever sold or merged. We'd tell you if that changed how your information is handled.

Cookies, analytics, and ads

We may use cookies, pixels, and similar tools, including Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking, and the LinkedIn Insight Tag. They tell us how people find and use the Site and whether our ads work. Advertising partners may use them to show you our ads on other sites, which some state laws call "targeted advertising" or "sharing."

You can limit this:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com and opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.
  • Manage LinkedIn ad preferences in your LinkedIn settings under Advertising data.
  • Use the opt-out tools at optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Email us and we'll help.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and fit-check information as long as we need it to follow up with you and handle the relationship, and for a reasonable period afterward for records, legal, and tax purposes. If you tell us to delete it, we will unless the law says we have to keep it.

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including access controls on the tools that hold your information. No method of sending or storing data is 100% secure, and we can't promise absolute security.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Virginia, and a growing list of other states), you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct it or delete it.
  • Opt out of targeted advertising, and of any sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights.

To use any of them, email shineyourlightonit@gmail.com. We may need to confirm it's you before we act. We'll respond within 45 days. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our response, and you can also contact your state attorney general.

In the past 12 months we collected identifiers (name, email, phone, IP address), professional information (business name, website, practice type), internet activity (pages viewed, referral source), and the answers you submitted in the Personality Match. We shared identifiers and internet activity with the advertising and analytics providers above. We did not sell personal information for money.

Children

The Site is for business owners and leaders. It isn't directed at anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

Where information is handled

We're based in the United States, and your information is processed here. If you visit from outside the US, you understand your information will be transferred to and handled in the US.

Client work is covered by its own agreements

This policy applies to the Site and to the information prospective clients send us. It does not govern protected health information we handle for a healthcare client. That is covered by the Business Associate Agreement and service agreement we sign with that client, and by HIPAA. If you are a patient of one of our clients and have a question about your records, please contact that practice directly.

Links to other sites

The Site links to other sites, like Calendly and LinkedIn. We're not responsible for their privacy practices. Read theirs.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If the change is a big one, we'll make it obvious on the Site.
