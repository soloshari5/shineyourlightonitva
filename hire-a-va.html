Questions

Before you book

Why not hire a $10–15/hour VA?

If you have the time to train, check and manage someone, that can work. Most of our clients come to us because they don't have that time. They need someone who takes ownership of the work, not more tasks to supervise.

Why a flat retainer instead of hourly?

Hourly billing rewards slow work and makes you watch the clock. A flat retainer means we own outcomes, and you always know what you're paying.

Are you really US-based?

Yes. Every team member is based in the US.

How do you keep my information confidential?

Every engagement starts with a signed NDA, secure credential handoff and role-based access. Healthcare clients also get a signed BAA.

What happens on the free call?

We look at where your time goes and find your biggest bottleneck. You leave with a short list of what to hand off first, even if you never work with us.

What if it isn't working?

Our 30-day transition guarantee means you're fully handed off within four weeks, or we make it right.
